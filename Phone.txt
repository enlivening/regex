Standardize Phone Numbers
-------------------------


To Format: +1XXXYYYZZZZ
---------
Search : (?<!\d)\+?(?:1[ \-]?)?(?<!\()(?:(?:\((\d{3})\))|(\d{3}))[ \-\/]?(\d{3})[ \-]?(\d{4})(?!\d)
Replace: +1$1$2$3$4


To Format: XXX-YYY-ZZZZ
---------
Search : (?<!\d)\+?(?:1[ \-]?)?(?<!\()(?:(?:\((\d{3})\))|(\d{3}))[ \-\/]?(\d{3})[ \-]?(\d{4})(?!\d)
Replace: $1$2-$3-$4


To Format: XXX.YYY.ZZZZ
---------
Search : (?<!\d)\+?(?:1[ \-]?)?(?<!\()(?:(?:\((\d{3})\))|(\d{3}))[ \-\/]?(\d{3})[ \-]?(\d{4})(?!\d)
Replace: $1$2.$3.$4


To Format: (XXX) YYY-ZZZZ
---------
Search : (?<!\d)\+?(?:1[ \-]?)?(?<!\()(?:(?:\((\d{3})\))|(\d{3}))[ \-\/]?(\d{3})[ \-]?(\d{4})(?!\d)
Replace: \($1$2\) $3-$4
