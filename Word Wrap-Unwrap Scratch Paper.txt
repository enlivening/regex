/* Unwrap at 120 chars */


Approach:
	1. Define Region.
	   a. Length of line <= 120 characters. (implies this line is valid)
	   b. Length of line + next word > 120 characters (implies next line is valid)
	   c. Region will usually and with double \R.
	   d. Longest possible unbroken sequence of such lines.
	2. Eliminate \R from section


/* 1a */	^.{1,120}
/* 1b */	(?=(?:.){120})



(?=^.{1,120}\R\S*?

All non-CRLF
	followed by
Single CRLF
	followed by
Exclusively non-space characters



(
	First word on next line qualification:
		(?:(?=^([\S\s]{120}))(?=[^\r\n]{1,120}(?=\R(?=\S*?(?<=^[\S\s]{120})))))

		OR

	Double line-ending qualification:
		(?=(?:[^\r\n]{1,120}\R){1,2}\R{2})
)

This line qualification:
	^.{1,120}$
